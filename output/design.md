# Campus Customs desk

The desk is a counter looking onto a Trinidad beach after carnival. The family is on the sand in the shop’s tees and hoodies. Doubles and bake-and-shark stands are up. The grown folks have a little rum. The tickets are still the work on the counter.

The page calls `http://localhost:8000`. The shop allows the Vite desk at `http://localhost:5173` to use those routes.

## Layout

The top is one continuous collage of real Trinidad and Tobago shores, not separate cards and not a drawing. Maracas Bay runs down the left side. Pigeon Point stretches across the top right, and Englishman’s Bay and the bake-and-shark stand share the lower right, edge to edge inside one rounded frame. Names and photo credits sit on the water itself, in a fade, so a caption bar never cuts the coast. Under that, “Campus Customs” sits with one line about the afternoon. Checking is a dark till, set apart so the balance is the number you see first. The three tickets are cards down the left. The right side is the ticket you picked, the button that starts the team, and five beach chairs.

On a narrow window the till, the tickets, and the chairs stack. The same pieces stay in that order.

## How the agents read

Each chair has its own towel so you can tell who is talking without reading a label twice. Boss is rum gold. Inventory is palm. Accounting is deep water. Facilities is sand. Customer Service is hibiscus. A chair that has started work turns brighter.

While the ticket runs, the chair shows what that person is saying and a plain line for what they are doing, taken from the tool they called: counting a size, reading the lease, looking at checking. When the run finishes, “What they did” repeats a short line for each person who actually worked that ticket. Chairs that stayed in the sand stay quiet. Not every ticket uses every person.

## Resolved tickets

A card says Open while the team has not finished it. When the run finishes, that card’s stamp changes to Resolved. The stamp is the green of the palm, so a finished ticket reads differently from an open one. You can still open it and read what each person did.

## How cash shows up

Checking lives in the till, from `cash_accounts`, with the shop date under it. Starting the team does not touch it. After a run, a ticket with a till move gets “Your approval.” On the Bulldog tee that line is the sale: list price comes in, and the reprint bill is not paid. On rent, that line is the check leaving. The button is the human yes. The till moves only after the click. The hoodie button fulfills 20 size M at 10% off, and it also pays the printer bill and the missing pieces so the rack can cover that size.
